# Payroll Administration

This context describes payroll-related concepts managed by the CONG back-office application.

## Employee Welfare Fund

**Employee Welfare Fund**:
The employee welfare fund (Thai: กองทุนสงเคราะห์ลูกจ้าง) funded by employee savings and employer contributions.
_Avoid_: Provident Fund, กองทุนสำรองเลี้ยงชีพ

**Employee Savings**:
The employee-funded portion of an Employee Welfare Fund contribution.
_Avoid_: Employee deduction

**Employer Contribution**:
The employer-funded portion of an Employee Welfare Fund contribution.
_Avoid_: Company savings
